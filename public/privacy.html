Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026.

Jammming is an independent educational portfolio app maintained by M-spina. Collection and use of information is governed by this policy. Spotify is not the operator of Jammming.

Fictional demo

The demo uses invented tracks, artists, and albums. Your demo searches, playlist name, selections, and simulated saved summary stay in page memory. They reset on reload or exit. Demo does not call Spotify.

Spotify mode

After you agree to the App Terms and authorize through Spotify, the app receives access and refresh tokens. It uses these to search Spotify and create explicitly public, shareable playlists containing the tracks you select. Search queries, playlist names, and chosen track identifiers are sent directly from your browser to Spotify. Spotify also receives connection information such as your IP address. The app requests user-read-private and playlist-modify-public scopes, and does not separately request your profile, email, or listening history.

Access/refresh tokens, expiry, authorization version, terms acceptance, and temporary OAuth state and PKCE verifier are stored in this tab's sessionStorage. OAuth state/verifier are consumed during login. Tokens may refresh while you use Spotify mode. Search results and the playlist draft are held in page memory. Tokens are readable by JavaScript on this origin; sessionStorage is not HttpOnly protection. It normally ends with the tab's browser session, but browser session restoration may retain it.

Deletion and disconnecting

Logout, entering demo, or an invalidated authorization clears the app's stored credentials and account-derived page state. Older localStorage credentials are removed on startup. If browser storage is unavailable, clear this site's data in browser settings. Logging out does not revoke Spotify's account-level permission or delete playlists already created there. Follow disconnect instructions to remove access, and manage or delete playlists in Spotify. Use Logout in other open Jammming tabs too.

Cookies, sharing, and hosting

Jammming sets no application cookies, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and has no application backend or account database. It does not intentionally share Spotify tokens with other recipients. Spotify operates its own service under its privacy policy. Visiting Spotify or GitHub links may involve those services' cookies and data collection.

Netlify, Inc. is the selected static hosting and content-delivery provider for public deployment. To deliver pages and operate and secure its infrastructure, Netlify processes connection and request information, which can include IP addresses, user-agent/browser information, requested URLs, request times, and response status. Request URLs can include the short-lived OAuth authorization code on arrival at /callback; clearing the browser URL does not erase a hosting log. Spotify access and refresh tokens, music search queries, and playlist contents are sent directly to Spotify, not to a Jammming backend on Netlify.

The deployment is intended to use static hosting without optional visitor analytics, Real User Monitoring, injected tracking scripts, Forms, Identity, or external log drains. These settings must be verified before public release; this policy must be updated if additional processing is enabled. Netlify's infrastructure logs are separate from the app's tab storage and cannot be deleted by Logout. The maintainer does not keep a separate copy of those logs. Netlify controls infrastructure-log retention under the applicable service and data-processing arrangements; no fixed retention period is promised here. See Netlify's Privacy Policy and data-protection information. Use your browser's site-data and cookie controls to manage browser storage.

Contact and rights

For privacy questions or requests about access, correction, deletion, or restriction, contact the maintainer through project issues. Issues are public: never post tokens, passwords, email addresses, account details, or other personal information. Describe the request in general terms. The app keeps no server-side Spotify account records; local deletion is available through Logout and browser site-data controls. If Spotify forwards a data-rights request, the maintainer will comply as required by its Developer Terms.

Spotify is a third-party beneficiary of this policy and the App Terms, and is entitled to directly enforce the App Terms.
